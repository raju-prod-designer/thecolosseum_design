theColosseum

Product structure · user journey & flow

Two connected arenas,
one AI-native hiring loop

Colosseum runs as two linked products. The company side is a web app for control over JDs, funnel visibility, candidate quality and evaluation. The candidate side is an AI-assisted flow for profile, screening, challenge-taking and matching. Both share one principle — infer first, ask only for what's missing.

Cross-cutting layer

The Arena — a gamified progression spine

Running underneath the candidate journey: a tier system that turns flat forms into a landscape the candidate climbs. Three.js was referenced as inspiration for making the flow feel immersive rather than sequential.

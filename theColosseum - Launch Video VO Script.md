## Tips

- **Voice settings:** Stability about 45–55% and Style about 20–30%. Pick a warm, assured voice that isn't too announcer-like.
- **Pronunciation:** Say "theColosseum" as "the Colosseum". If the voice misreads "JD", write it as "J.D.".
- **Exact sync:** For tighter sync, generate each line as its own clip and place it at its Start time in your editor.
- **Retiming:** If you retime scenes on the video timeline, shift each line by the same amount as its scene.

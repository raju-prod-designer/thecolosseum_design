Work as Evidence

Why a cumulative record of solved problems, not the résumé, is the asset we are building.

Companies should be able to hire or engage people based on proven work. Talent should be able to prove capability by solving real problems and getting paid for the result.

theColosseum · by AudenaConfidential

01 · Two signals

What hiring measures today, and what it could measure

Credentials

A claim about ability

Titles, years of experience, schools, keywords. Cheap to produce, hard to verify, and filtered before anyone looks at what the person can actually do.

Evidence

A record of work performed

A real problem from a real company, a stated effort cap, a public rubric, a scored result and a paid outcome. Verifiable by anyone who reads it.

The premise

Credentials describe what someone says they did. Evidence shows what they did, against a brief someone else wrote.

02 · Where the current process breaks

Better screening alone does not fix hiring

The problem sits in the unit being evaluated. Three failures follow from evaluating the proxy.

Proxy

The wrong people are filtered out

Résumé filters reject capable people who describe themselves poorly and pass people who describe themselves well.

Unpaid

Take-homes are a hidden tax

Candidates do hours of unpaid work into silence. The best ones are the least willing to do it.

One-sided

Only one party is accountable

Companies rate candidates. Candidates have no public record of vague briefs, moving scope or slow reviews.

03 · The central concept

The asset is the reputation loop

Every completed challenge adds to a talent's standing. Standing decides which opportunities they see and what those opportunities pay.

1

Real problem

A company posts a funded challenge. The reward is held in escrow before work starts.

2

Work delivered

Talent solves it inside a stated effort cap and submission window.

3

Scored with reasons

Rubric scores, quoted evidence, company feedback and a released payment.

4

Standing updated

Rank, tier and reward multipliers move. Higher tiers unlock larger prizes.

The principle

A résumé can be rewritten in an afternoon. A record of paid, reviewed work can only be earned.

05 · Compounding

Reputation is cumulative and visible

Input

Completed challenges

Each finished brief is a public, scored entry on the profile.

Input

Earned payments

Money released by a company is the hardest signal to fake.

Input

Company feedback

Ratings and reviews from the teams the work was done for.

Input

Contribution

How the problem was solved, shared and referenced by others.

Quality outranks volume

Higher-quality results move ranking further than more submissions. Standing is weighted toward the best work, not the most.

Accountability runs both ways

Companies carry an Employer score and public ratings on brief clarity, review speed and payment behaviour.

06 · The social layer

Work that people can see, discuss and learn from

The product should stay simple and social. Solved problems become something to share, which brings skilled people in and keeps them returning.

Feed

How a problem was solved, posted by the person who solved it.

Badges

Recognition for placements, streaks and difficult challenges.

Comments

Discussion on approach, from peers and from the posting company.

Collaborative challenges

Larger problems solved by small teams, with shared credit.

Status

Under exploration. These elements are candidates, to be tested once the core loop has live users.

07 · AI as the core experience

AI-first on both sides of the market

For companies · finding people, solving problems

  • Drafts challengesfrom the company's website and product context
  • Screens submissionsagainst hard gates and weighted criteria, with quotes
  • Runs interviewscovering hard and soft skills, scored and transcribed
  • Flags gamingand sends it to human review

For talent · finding work, completing it

  • Builds the profilefrom GitHub, LinkedIn or portfolio links
  • Matches opportunitiesto skills, tier and preferences
  • Explains outcomeswith the stage, the bar and the evidence
  • Supports the workitself, as a possible next step
The constraint

AI shows its reasoning, its evidence and its unknowns at every step. A verdict without reasons is not accepted from it.

08 · Customer zero

Audena posts first

Audena will post its own hiring needs and paid challenges on theColosseum. This tests the full product with real incentives and real outcomes before any external company is asked to.

Small

Scoped tasks

Short, well-defined problems that exercise posting, submission, scoring and payout end to end.

Large

Technical problems

Substantial engineering work, such as observability, where the result matters to Audena's own product.

Roles

Real hiring needs

Open positions filled through hiring challenges, tested against Audena's own bar.

Why

If the loop does not produce useful work and good hires for its own builders, it will not for anyone else.

Now

10 · Operating principle

Outcomes over complexity

An 80/20 approach: keep infrastructure and operations lean, test what produces results, and defer anything that is decoration until the product has market proof.

Build now

  • Post, fund, publishfor challenges, bounties and jobs
  • Escrow and payoutthat actually moves money
  • Scoring with reasonsand a visible rank
  • One launch channel

Defer until proven

  • Secondary integrationsincluding LinkedIn
  • Full social layerbeyond what drives return visits
  • Team challengesand shared credit
  • AI work assistancefor talent
